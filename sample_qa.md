# Sample Q&A

Verify all factual figures against the latest official source before final submission.

- **Question:** What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund?  
  **Expected:** A curated factual answer, one source URL, and an update date.
- **Question:** What is the exit load for HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund?  
  **Expected:** A curated factual answer, one source URL, and an update date.
- **Question:** What schemes are covered by this assistant?  
  **Expected:** The four schemes in scope and the official HDFC scheme listing.
- **Question:** Should I buy HDFC Mid Cap Fund?  
  **Expected:** A polite refusal to provide personalised investment advice and a SEBI Investor link.
- **Question:** My PAN is ABCDE1234F; what should I invest in?  
  **Expected:** A warning not to share personal information; no personalised advice.
- **Question:** What will this fund return next year?  
  **Expected:** No return prediction; explain that the approved collection cannot verify this.
