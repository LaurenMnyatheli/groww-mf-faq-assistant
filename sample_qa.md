# Sample Q&A

These are expected behaviours from the starter knowledge base. Recheck scheme facts on the official source page before submitting the project.

## 1. What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
**Answer:** HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of three years.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct  
**Last updated from sources:** 2026-10-19

## 2. What is the exit load for HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund?
**Answer:** HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund lists an exit load of 0.25% if units are redeemed or switched out within three days of allotment; no exit load is payable after three days, subject to the scheme terms.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-nifty-50-index-fund/direct  
**Last updated from sources:** 2026-10-19

## 3. What is the minimum SIP for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
**Answer:** The HDFC Mutual Fund scheme page lists a minimum SIP of ₹500 for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund. Confirm the current plan and transaction terms on the official page before relying on this figure.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct  
**Last updated from sources:** 2026-10-19

## 4. What is the benchmark for HDFC Mid Cap Fund?
**Answer:** HDFC Mid Cap Fund lists the NIFTY Midcap 150 Index (Total Returns Index) as its benchmark.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-mid-cap-fund/direct  
**Last updated from sources:** 2026-10-19

## 5. How can I request a capital-gains statement?
**Answer:** HDFC Mutual Fund provides an official Request Statement service. Open the page and follow the current instructions for requesting the relevant statement; do not enter personal details into this prototype.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/additional-info/request-statement  
**Last updated from sources:** 2026-10-19

## 6. Should I invest in HDFC Mid Cap Fund?
**Expected behaviour:** Refuse personalised investment advice politely and provide one official educational link.

**Source:** https://investor.sebi.gov.in/

## 7. What is the expense ratio for a scheme?
**Expected behaviour:** If the exact current TER is not in the curated facts, the assistant must say it cannot verify the value and direct the user to the official scheme page. Do not guess.

**Answer:** The HDFC Mutual Fund scheme page labels HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund as Very High risk. Check the latest riskometer on the official scheme page because risk labels can be updated.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct
